## usage-band 0.1.0 (2026-10-08)

### Added

- A band above the prompt with the 5-hour and 7-day rate limits, context fill, session tokens and cost.
- Pace warnings: a limit turns yellow when the current pace would hit it before it resets, and red when it is close.
- `/usage-pill` to refresh and print a summary, hide or show the band, and choose when the cost pill shows.
- Terminal and desktop app drawing, fitted to the window width.
